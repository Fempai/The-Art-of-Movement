Dance pathways

Isolation & musicality

Belly dance hip slides, figure eights, ribcage circles, accents and rhythm.

Sensual floorwork

Heels & performance

Balance, posture, walking technique, safe turns and choreography phrasing.

Burlesque & showgirl

Stage presence, character, gesture, theatricality and musical storytelling.

Twerk & glute isolations

Pelvic tilts, hip control, rhythm and progressive lower-body endurance.

Embodiment, confidence, attention and self-directed reflection. Spiritual practices are optional, never required.
