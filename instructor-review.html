Assessment rubric

Future AI reviews will score alignment and control, balance, timing and musicality, repeatability, and discipline-specific technique. The instructor will provide time-stamped observations, actionable drills, and a provisional skill level.

Levels: Foundations → Developing Beginner → Intermediate Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Artistry. Each discipline progresses independently. Reviews must distinguish visible evidence from uncertainty, and should not claim to detect internal muscle activation or diagnose injuries.

High-risk acrobatic or contortion progressions will not be automatically cleared by AI review alone, regardless of score.
