Technique Calibration Lab

A workspace for developing instructor-defined technique criteria and checking the evidence needed before an automated grade could be justified. Automated grades are currently disabled. Pose matching, 3D skeletal estimates, and five-second holds cannot establish correct dance technique. Nothing here uploads your files.

1. Define the technique rubric

Each criterion is instructor-scored from 0 to 4. Weights must total 1. Measured criteria describe potential camera-derived inputs; they do not automatically generate instructor-equivalent grades.

2. Review instructor-labeled evidence

Use a locally stored JSON dataset with explicit training/validation assignments. Each performer must appear in only one split. Validation requires independent performers, multiple instructors, and multiple camera setups. These are minimum research gates, not evidence that a scoring model is accurate.

No dataset evaluated. Automated grading is unavailable.

3. Grading policy

Not calibrated: no numerical grade or pass/fail decision will be issued.

Before enabling grading, the Academy needs an instructor-reviewed skill rubric, diverse consented and annotated performances, independently held-out evaluation, agreement across raters, repeatability under camera changes, safety review, and predeclared acceptance thresholds. This lab intentionally cannot bypass those requirements.
