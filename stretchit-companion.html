Term I plan

Start with Full Body Intro (14 days) as the proposed primary program for Weeks 1–2, subject to reviewing its actual class schedule and your recovery. Later programs are provisional and should not be rushed to fit calendar weeks. The daily gentle mobility warm-up remains separate. Do not force splits or deep backbends to match the app's challenges.

For Week 3, focus on controlled hips, hamstrings, calves, shoulders, and comfortable thoracic mobility. Select 10–25 minute beginner sessions that suit your recovery, not advanced performance-flexibility challenges.
