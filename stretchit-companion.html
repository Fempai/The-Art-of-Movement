Term I plan

Weeks 1–3: choose Difficulty 1 or Absolute Beginner sessions. Start with two or three guided classes each week, spaced around gym and dance recovery. The daily gentle mobility warm-up remains separate. Do not force splits or deep backbends to match the app's challenges.

For Week 3, focus on controlled hips, hamstrings, calves, shoulders, and comfortable thoracic mobility. Select 10–25 minute beginner sessions that suit your recovery, not advanced performance-flexibility challenges.
