## Evidence collection protocol

For each scheduled source, capture the date, device/browser, viewer permission (without sharing credentials), whether the file opens, whether playback starts, whether seeking works, and the time range that illustrates the named movement. Record the actual observation and any error; do not infer success from the URL or file title. Compare the observed movement to the corresponding lesson's counts and safety modifications, documenting agreements, mismatches, and any unsupported cultural claims. Mark each status **Verified**, **Failed**, or **Not verified** only after collecting evidence. One reported successful belly-dance video playback on 2026-11-02 cannot be attributed to a specific catalog item.

A separate deployed-reader check must verify link opening, keyboard/mobile access, quiz submission, answer explanations, and progress persistence. These are not covered by this source ledger or by text-only CI.
