# Dance Studio product scope and safety gates

## Curriculum tracks (planned, not yet launched)

- Belly dance: hip slides, circles, figure eights, shimmies and musicality.
- Sensual floorwork and low-flow transitions: kneeling, seated, floor pathways, controlled rises; protect knees and wrists.
- Heels technique: ankle strength, balance, walking, pivots, musicality, then choreographed combinations.
- Burlesque and showgirl: performance quality, expression, theatricality, musical phrasing, stage presence.
- Feminine expression: optional self-reflection on confidence, embodiment, identity and cultural/spiritual traditions without prescribing gender norms or spiritual beliefs.
- Twerk and glute isolation: pelvic control, rhythm, conditioning and graded endurance.
- Contemporary, jazz, ballet-inspired, hip-hop, breaking and acro: distinct prerequisite tracks.

## Studio experience (planned)

- Video player: speed selection (0.5x, 0.75x, 1x), loop section, mirrored playback when supported, chapter markers and captioned cues.
- Performance mode: distraction-free fullscreen practice and cue countdown.
- Choreography library: eight-count breakdowns, practice loops, combination progressions, bookmarks and completion history.
- Coaching review: user-submitted videos in **private** storage, explicit sharing permission, rubric-based technique notes, corrections and approval states. No automated claim of injury risk diagnosis or verified movement accuracy from computer vision.
- Progress timeline: dated videos and photos, opt-in side-by-side comparison, user-controlled retrospective montage, deletion and export. Never public by default.
- Instructor videos: must be real and technique-verified; synthetic avatar can introduce lessons but should not be the sole source for demonstrating complex biomechanics.
