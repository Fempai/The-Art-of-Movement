Instructor-Curated Class Pathways

A structured shortlist of real classes selected for Level I teaching objectives, not random choreography links. Each class has a specific role in its discipline's progression.

Access and rights: These are links to creators' own websites and players. Free viewing does not authorize us to copy, remove advertisements or cloud-host a class. Paid classes are labeled. This is an initial research-based shortlist, not a hands-on video quality audit; class playback, cue quality, camera angles, captions and exact access need final verification before formal academy accreditation.
