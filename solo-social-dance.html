TERM I / OPTIONAL CULTURAL & ROLE STUDIES

Solo salsa, bachata & tango

Practice the rhythm, footwork, and decision-making of both leader and follower roles without a partner. These are introductory written drills, not substitutes for an instructor, a style-specific technique class, or consensual partner practice.

Curriculum timing: Salsa begins in Week 9, bachata in Week 10, tango in Week 11, and role-switching integration in Week 13. These optional studies do not replace the original core styles or gym plan. For tango, distinguish Argentine social tango from ballroom tango; this module introduces only general axis and walking concepts.
