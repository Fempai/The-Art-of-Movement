Technique rubric · observation, not certification

Evaluate each dimension separately: alignment (joint stacking and comfortable range), control (stable entry and exit), timing (relationship to an audible count), coordination (consistent sequencing), and recovery (no lingering pain or excessive fatigue). Use the same task and camera position when comparing attempts. Mark anything you cannot see or hear as not observed, not as a failure.

Evidence confidence: Self-reflection = personal perception; Instructor review = feedback actually provided by a qualified person; Experimental pose measurement = limited 2D landmarks, not a technique grade; AI feedback = unvalidated suggestion. None automatically unlock acro or advanced flexibility.
