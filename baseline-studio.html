Movement Baseline Studio

Build a private photographic and video record of posture, flexibility, active control and dance technique. Compare like-for-like views over time. These are observational baselines, not automated diagnoses or proficiency grades.

Private uploads are stored in the academy's protected Supabase Storage bucket and indexed under your account. Media is shown through short-lived signed links. For consistent comparisons, keep the same camera angle, distance, warm-up and movement where possible. Do not upload anyone else's footage without their permission.
