START DATE · MONDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2026

Weekday strength + dance

5:00 AM wake, brush teeth, splash face, vitamin C, feed dogs. Leave by 5:30 AM. Gym around 5:35 AM; finish and freshen up before work at 7:00 AM.

Daily order: BFR strength (15–20 min) → treadmill (5–10 min) → dynamic mobility (5 min) → dance (20–25 min) → flexibility (8–10 min). Total about 53–70 minutes, adjusted to your commute and changing time.

BFR safety: Use properly fitted cuffs and individualized limb-occlusion pressure under qualified guidance; do not guess pressure or tighten bands by feel. Avoid BFR without medical clearance if you have a history of blood clots, significant vascular/cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, or other relevant contraindications. Stop immediately for numbness, unusual pain, dizziness, discoloration or breathlessness. The 30/15/15/15 scheme is a common option, not a requirement; take longer rests or use ordinary light resistance instead if needed. Never use BFR cuffs for treadmill or dance.
