# TWK201 · Lesson 07 — Multi-Pattern Phrase and Accent Mapping

**Standalone twerk technique | learner-facing written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** TWK101 foundation control recommended; not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Combine rebounds, side shifts, hinge and accent placement in a repeatable sixteen-count study.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original coordination drill within a twerk dance pathway, not a claim about a universal historical step. Black social dance and New Orleans bounce context require verified citations and practitioner review before publication as authoritative instruction.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes and rehearse one comfortable eight-count from TWK201-01, -02 and -03. Phrase A counts 1–2 shallow rebound, 3–4 recover, 5–6 shift right and return, 7–8 shift left and return. Phrase B counts 1–2 small supported hinge and return, 3–4 shallow rebound, 5–6 shift right and return, 7–8 return upright and pause. Practice A–B twice with rests, then repeat while accenting count 5 of Phrase A. Keep range modest and breathe.

## Accessible alternative
Use a stable chair to mark small pressure shifts and torso inclinations. Note that seated versions change standing mechanics and support demands.

## Self-correction and safety
Lost count: practice Phrase A alone. Back strain: omit hinge. Balance loss: use counter and shorter shifts. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Notate A and B, label the changed accent and explain one chosen adaptation.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. How many counts are in the full A–B study?**

A. Sixteen

B. Four

C. Eight

D. Thirty-two

**Key: A.** Sixteen.

**2. Where is the optional new accent placed?**

A. On every subdivision

B. On count 5 of Phrase A

C. Only at the end of Phrase B

D. On a forced jump

**Key: B.** On count 5 of Phrase A.

**3. What is the first correction when counts are lost?**

A. Add tempo

B. Increase complexity

C. Practice Phrase A alone

D. Ignore the phrase boundary

**Key: C.** Practice Phrase A alone.

**4. What should the learner describe in the assignment?**

A. Only total repetitions

B. Only the music title

C. Only squat depth

D. The two phrases, accent and adaptation

**Key: D.** The two phrases, accent and adaptation.

## Completion checkpoint
Reproduce or notate two comfortable phrases and explain the movement initiator, one correction and a timing choice. Written completion does not prove physical proficiency.
