# TWK201 · Lesson 05 — Squat Depth and Endurance Choices

**Standalone twerk technique | learner-facing written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** TWK101 foundation control recommended; not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Choose a sustainable range and rest schedule instead of treating deep squats as a prerequisite.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original coordination drill within a twerk dance pathway, not a claim about a universal historical step. Black social dance and New Orleans bounce context require verified citations and practitioner review before publication as authoritative instruction.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with five minutes of walking, small knee bends and gentle lateral shifts. Practice an eight-count shallow bounce twice, then rest for at least one eight-count. Repeat three sets at a conversational pace. In a second study, reduce the range by half while keeping the same counts and compare fatigue and control. Never require a deep squat, painful knee position or sustained breath-holding.

## Accessible alternative
Light counter support or seated rhythmic pressure changes; these maintain timing but not the same standing muscular load.

## Self-correction and safety
Fatigue causes form changes: rest or shorten the set. Knee discomfort: stop or reduce depth. Unsteady balance: use stable support. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Compare two depths using counts, comfort, and control rather than a maximum repetition score.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is the lesson's priority when selecting squat depth?**

A. Comfortable repeatable control

B. Maximum depth regardless of pain

C. Speed over balance

D. A mandatory floor drop

**Key: A.** Comfortable repeatable control.

**2. What follows two eight-count phrases in the first study?**

A. A jump

B. A rest of at least one eight-count

C. A forced deep squat

D. A backbend

**Key: B.** A rest of at least one eight-count.

**3. If fatigue changes control, what should happen?**

A. Increase intensity

B. Ignore fatigue

C. Rest or shorten the set

D. Lock the knees

**Key: C.** Rest or shorten the set.

**4. What does seated rhythmic marking not duplicate?**

A. Count placement

B. Listening to accents

C. Small pressure changes

D. Standing muscular loading

**Key: D.** Standing muscular loading.

## Completion checkpoint
Reproduce or notate two comfortable phrases and explain the movement initiator, one correction and a timing choice. Written completion does not prove physical proficiency.

## Production gate
Authorized technique-reviewed instructor demonstration, front/side views, captions and browser playback acceptance are required before video-course release.
