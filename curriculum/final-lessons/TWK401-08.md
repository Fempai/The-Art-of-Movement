# TWK401 · Lesson 08 — Recorded Capstone and Reflection

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Present an intentional original performance and evaluate observable choices without claiming certified mastery.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes, then choose your 32-count choreography from Lesson 07. Perform it twice with rest, selecting a comfortable standing or seated adaptation. If recording, use a private device and verify camera framing and consent for anyone appearing; do not upload private recordings by default. If not recording, submit a written score and self-observation instead. Compare timing, recovery, chosen accents and transitions, not unobservable muscle activation.

## Accessible alternative
A written or seated capstone is equally acceptable as an assignment pathway, while acknowledging different movement mechanics.

## Self-correction and safety
If discomfort arises, stop. If framing obscures an action, label it unobservable rather than incorrect. If recording privacy is uncertain, use written reflection. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Submit a 32-count score or optional private recording, two observable strengths, one correction, and one cultural-context limitation.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is required for capstone submission?**

A. A 32-count score or optional private recording with reflection

B. A public video upload

C. Maximum squat depth

D. An expert certification claim

**Key: A.** A 32-count score or optional private recording with reflection is the supported answer.

**2. How should obscured movement in a recording be described?**

A. Automatically incorrect

B. Unobservable from the framing

C. Guaranteed correct

D. Evidence of muscle activation

**Key: B.** Unobservable from the framing is the supported answer.

**3. What is the default privacy expectation for recordings?**

A. Automatically public

B. Uploaded without consent

C. Private unless explicitly shared

D. Shared with all Academy users

**Key: C.** Private unless explicitly shared is the supported answer.

**4. What must the reflection include?**

A. Only a completion badge

B. Only a music title

C. Only repetition count

D. Two strengths, one correction and a cultural-context limitation

**Key: D.** Two strengths, one correction and a cultural-context limitation is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
