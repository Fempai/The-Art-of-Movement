# TWK401 · Lesson 03 — Dynamics and Texture

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Contrast gentle continuous motion with deliberately separated accents while retaining safe range.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with gentle walking and small pulses. Phrase A: perform four continuous shallow pulses over eight counts with smooth returns. Phrase B: use the same movement pathway but add deliberate pauses on counts 4 and 8. Repeat A–B twice, resting between sets. Compare whether the phrase feels flowing or punctuated without increasing depth or speed.

## Accessible alternative
Seated small pressure changes and hand accents retain contrast in texture, not standing load.

## Self-correction and safety
If pauses cause bracing, keep breathing and reduce range. If knee discomfort appears, switch to seated. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Record two descriptions of the same pathway using different dynamic choices.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is varied between Phrase A and B?**

A. Dynamic texture and pauses

B. Required joint range

C. Music speed

D. Floor height

**Key: A.** Dynamic texture and pauses is the supported answer.

**2. Which counts pause in Phrase B?**

A. 2 and 6

B. 4 and 8

C. 1 and 5

D. 3 and 7

**Key: B.** 4 and 8 is the supported answer.

**3. What should happen if a pause causes breath-holding?**

A. Hold longer

B. Increase intensity

C. Breathe and reduce range

D. Add a squat

**Key: C.** Breathe and reduce range is the supported answer.

**4. Which quality best describes Phrase A?**

A. Only sharp accents

B. A compulsory stop

C. A high-impact jump

D. Smooth continuous motion

**Key: D.** Smooth continuous motion is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.
