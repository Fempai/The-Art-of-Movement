# TWK301 · Lesson 06 — Floorwork Alternatives and Joint Protection

**Standalone advanced twerk pathway | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count phrase. Not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Select low-level movement options that respect knee, wrist and back comfort.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original movement study, not a named historical step. Modern twerk is associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; detailed cultural claims need verified sources and practitioner review before authoritative publication.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up gently while standing. Practice a seated eight-count phrase on a stable chair: 1–2 small pelvic rock and return; 3–4 pressure shift right and center; 5–6 left and center; 7–8 two hand accents. Optionally repeat on a supportive floor mat only if getting down and up is comfortable; avoid prolonged pressure on knees or wrists. Rest and compare the two support conditions. This is an accessible floorwork study, not a requirement to kneel.

## Accessible alternative
Use the chair throughout; maintain musical timing and intentional pelvic movement without floor contact.

## Self-correction and safety
If knees or wrists hurt, return to chair. If low back pinches, reduce pelvic rock. If fatigue rises, shorten phrase. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Assignment
Compare chair and floor support in a written eight-count map, naming one mechanical difference.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is the primary safe alternative to floor practice?**

A. Stable chair study

B. Forced kneeling

C. Deep squat hold

D. Wrist-loaded plank

**Key: A.** Stable chair study is correct for the exercise.

**2. What happens on counts 3–4?**

A. A jump

B. Rightward pressure shift and return

C. A backbend

D. A turn

**Key: B.** Rightward pressure shift and return is correct for the exercise.

**3. Which cue applies if low back pinches?**

A. Increase rock range

B. Add speed

C. Reduce the pelvic rock or stop

D. Ignore discomfort

**Key: C.** Reduce the pelvic rock or stop is correct for the exercise.

**4. What must the reflection acknowledge?**

A. All surfaces are identical

B. Chair and floor have equal loading

C. Floorwork is mandatory

D. Support conditions change joint demands

**Key: D.** Support conditions change joint demands is correct for the exercise.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate two comfortable phrases, explain one timing choice and identify a correction. Written responses do not certify technique mastery.
