# TWK301 · Lesson 01 — Layered Accents

**Standalone twerk advanced movement | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended prerequisite: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count combination. Not a validated instructor-led video.

## Objective
Maintain a stable lower-body pulse while adding a distinct upper-body or hand accent without accelerating the base rhythm.

## Cultural context
This original exercise explores musical and physical coordination. Twerk's relationships with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce warrant verified sources and practitioner review; no universal traditional step is claimed here.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes with easy walking, shallow pulses and comfortable shoulder mobility. Set a steady eight-count shallow pulse. Phrase A: counts 1–8 maintain the same pulse with relaxed arms. Phrase B: keep identical lower-body timing, adding a gentle hand accent on counts 2 and 6. Repeat A–B twice with breaks. Then swap the hand accent to counts 4 and 8 while preserving the same lower-body phrase. Practice 10–15 minutes; do not force rapid spinal or pelvic snaps.

## Accessible alternative
Seated pressure shifts with hand accents preserve layering and counting but not standing loading.

## Self-correction and safety
If pulse speeds up, remove hand accents and reestablish counts. If shoulders tense, reduce arm range. If balance changes, use stable counter support. Stop for pain, dizziness or loss of balance.

## Assignment
Notate two eight-count versions with different accent maps; describe which body action stayed constant.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What stays constant between Phrase A and Phrase B?**

A. Lower-body pulse timing

B. Hand accent placement

C. Shoulder shape

D. The need for maximum depth

**Key: A.** Lower-body pulse timing.

**2. Which counts receive hand accents in the first Phrase B?**

A. 1 and 5

B. 2 and 6

C. 3 and 7

D. 4 and 8

**Key: B.** 2 and 6.

**3. What should happen if layering speeds up the pulse?**

A. Increase range

B. Add more gestures

C. Remove hand accents and reestablish counts

D. Hold breath

**Key: C.** Remove hand accents and reestablish counts.

**4. Which seated statement is accurate?**

A. Identical to standing load

B. Proof of isolated muscle activation

C. A required floor variation

D. Timing is retained but standing loading changes

**Key: D.** Timing is retained but standing loading changes.

## Completion checkpoint
Demonstrate or notate two intentional eight-count phrases, explain the difference, and document one correction. Written work does not certify physical proficiency.
