# TWK401 · Lesson 04 — Transitions and Stage Orientation

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Use simple directional choices and clear starts/stops without unsafe turns.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with walking and easy weight shifts. Face forward for counts 1–4, making two small pulses. On counts 5–6 take a small side step and gather while maintaining comfortable orientation. Counts 7–8 pause and face front again. Repeat with the side step in the opposite direction. If turning is comfortable, replace only the pause with a tiny step-based orientation adjustment, never a planted-knee twist.

## Accessible alternative
Use seated hand gestures to indicate left/right stage direction while keeping the trunk comfortable; no standing travel required.

## Self-correction and safety
If disoriented, keep facing forward. If a knee twists, shorten step and move feet together rather than pivoting on a planted knee. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Sketch the eight-count stage pathway and mark where you face forward.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What happens on counts 5–6?**

A. A small side step and gather

B. A forced spin

C. A floor drop

D. A jump

**Key: A.** A small side step and gather is the supported answer.

**2. How should an optional orientation change occur?**

A. Twist on a planted knee

B. Use small steps to adjust direction

C. Arch the back

D. Close the eyes

**Key: B.** Use small steps to adjust direction is the supported answer.

**3. What is a safe choice if disoriented?**

A. Turn faster

B. Add travel

C. Remain facing forward

D. Increase range

**Key: C.** Remain facing forward is the supported answer.

**4. What should the stage map show?**

A. Only the song title

B. Only calories

C. Only repetitions

D. Direction and forward-facing moments

**Key: D.** Direction and forward-facing moments is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
