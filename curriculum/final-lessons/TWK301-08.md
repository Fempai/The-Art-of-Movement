# TWK301 · Lesson 08 — Advanced Combination and Reflection

**Standalone advanced twerk pathway | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count phrase. Not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Compose and repeat a thirty-two-count combination using timing, direction and a chosen level option.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original movement study, not a named historical step. Modern twerk is associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; detailed cultural claims need verified sources and practitioner review before authoritative publication.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes and review comfortable versions of directional steps, layered accents and shallow level changes. Phrase A (1–8): step right, gather, pulse, return, step left, gather, pulse, return. Phrase B (9–16): shallow hinge and return twice, with a hand accent on count 12. Phrase C (17–24): repeat A with smaller steps. Phrase D (25–32): four counts of small pulse and four counts of recovery. Practice each eight-count segment alone, then join them at a conversational pace twice with breaks.

## Accessible alternative
Keep movements in place with counter support or use seated directional reaches, pressure shifts and accents. State which traveling or level-change mechanics were replaced.

## Self-correction and safety
If counts are lost, practice segments separately. If a hinge causes discomfort, omit it. If fatigued, rehearse only two segments. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Assignment
Submit a 32-count score or optional private recording with one correction, one musical decision and an adaptation.
