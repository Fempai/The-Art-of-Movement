# TWK201 · Lesson 06 — Supported Hands-on-Thighs Positions

**Standalone twerk technique | learner-facing written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** TWK101 foundation control recommended; not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Use a modest hip hinge and light hand support without collapsing through the back or loading wrists excessively.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original coordination drill within a twerk dance pathway, not a claim about a universal historical step. Black social dance and New Orleans bounce context require verified citations and practitioner review before publication as authoritative instruction.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with easy walking, shallow hinges and soft-knee standing. With hands resting lightly above the knees on the thighs, practice counts 1–2 small leg-led pulse; 3–4 recover; 5–6 small weight shift right and center; 7–8 left and center. Return upright between phrases. Practice four rounds with breaks. Keep knees tracking with feet; avoid forced lumbar extension and leaning body weight heavily onto hands.

## Accessible alternative
Practice upright with a counter for light balance support; seated small torso shifts can preserve the rhythmic map but not standing hip-hinge forces.

## Self-correction and safety
Wrist or hand pressure: raise torso and reduce support loading. Back discomfort: return upright. Knee twist: decrease lateral range. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Compare upright and supported versions, identifying one benefit and one mechanical difference.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. Where should hands rest in this study?**

A. Lightly on the thighs above the knees

B. On an unstable chair

C. On the lower back while arching

D. Under the feet

**Key: A.** Lightly on the thighs above the knees.

**2. What should you do if hands carry excessive body weight?**

A. Push harder

B. Raise torso and reduce loading

C. Increase hinge depth

D. Lock elbows

**Key: B.** Raise torso and reduce loading.

**3. Which movement is scheduled on counts 5–6?**

A. A jump

B. A floor drop

C. Small rightward weight shift and return

D. A full turn

**Key: C.** Small rightward weight shift and return.

**4. What does the seated option change?**

A. Only the song

B. Only the count

C. Nothing mechanical

D. Standing hip-hinge and support forces

**Key: D.** Standing hip-hinge and support forces.

## Completion checkpoint
Reproduce or notate two comfortable phrases and explain the movement initiator, one correction and a timing choice. Written completion does not prove physical proficiency.
