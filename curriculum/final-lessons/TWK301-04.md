# TWK301 · Lesson 04 — Level Changes Without Forced Depth

**Standalone advanced twerk pathway | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count phrase. Not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Transition between upright and shallow hinged positions while maintaining balance and breath.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original movement study, not a named historical step. Modern twerk is associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; detailed cultural claims need verified sources and practitioner review before authoritative publication.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with easy walking, hip hinges and ankle motion. Counts 1–2 stay upright with shallow pulse; 3–4 hinge slightly at hips and return; 5–6 repeat upright pulse; 7–8 recover in neutral. Repeat twice with counter nearby, then add a gentle musical accent at the start of the hinge. Rest between sets. Avoid deep squat demands.

## Accessible alternative
Remain upright for all counts with hand accents, or use a stable chair for small torso inclinations; neither replicates standing level change.

## Self-correction and safety
Back discomfort: omit hinge. Loss of balance: use counter. Knee pain: reduce bend depth. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Assignment
Score the phrase and explain the difference between a shallow hinge and a forced spinal arch.

**3. What is a suitable response to back discomfort?**

A. Increase arch

B. Speed up

C. Omit the hinge and return upright

D. Hold longer

**Key: C.** Omit the hinge and return upright is correct for the exercise.

**4. Which statement about seated torso inclinations is accurate?**

A. Identical to standing

B. Proof of muscular isolation

C. A required squat

D. Different loading from standing level changes

**Key: D.** Different loading from standing level changes is correct for the exercise.
