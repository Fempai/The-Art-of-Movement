# TWK201 · Lesson 08 — Technique Evaluation and Controlled Combination

**Standalone twerk technique | learner-facing written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** TWK101 foundation control recommended; not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Demonstrate two contrasting sixteen-count phrases with clear movement initiators and self-correction decisions.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original coordination drill within a twerk dance pathway, not a claim about a universal historical step. Black social dance and New Orleans bounce context require verified citations and practitioner review before publication as authoritative instruction.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes, then select one small rebound, one right/left weight shift and one comfortable hinge or upright substitute. Build Phrase A: counts 1–4 rebound and recovery, 5–8 lateral shifts. Build Phrase B: counts 1–4 hinge and return or an upright alternative, 5–8 rebound with a chosen accent. Practice slowly twice and at a comfortable pulse twice with rests. Evaluate timing, breath, balance and repeatability; do not treat video appearance as proof of specific muscle activation.

## Accessible alternative
Counter-supported or seated timing version; document which standing actions are changed or omitted rather than claiming equivalence.

## Self-correction and safety
If timing breaks, simplify to eight counts. If discomfort occurs, stop and reduce range. If recording framing obscures pelvis, do not infer invisible mechanics. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Submit a sixteen-count written score or optional private recording, one observation, one correction and one cultural-attribution boundary.

## Completion checkpoint
Reproduce or notate two comfortable phrases and explain the movement initiator, one correction and a timing choice. Written completion does not prove physical proficiency.

## Production gate
Authorized technique-reviewed instructor demonstration, front/side views, captions and browser playback acceptance are required before video-course release.
