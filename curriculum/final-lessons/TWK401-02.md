# TWK401 · Lesson 02 — Bounce Music Listening and Context

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Identify pulse and accent patterns while separating observation from unsupported historical attribution.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Choose a legally accessible music excerpt appropriate for personal practice; no copyrighted audio is included. Listen for a recurring pulse and mark eight numbered beats by hand. Listen again and note two audible accents without assuming they are always offbeats. Then perform a comfortable small-range pulse for two eight-count phrases, placing a hand accent where you hear a strong musical event. Rest, repeat and compare your notation with what you hear.

## Accessible alternative
Use hand taps and seated pressure shifts only; rhythmic listening does not require standing.

## Self-correction and safety
If the beat is unclear, slow the practice count without altering the recording or use a simpler authorized excerpt. Do not invent an artist's origin or song history. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Write an eight-count listening map and distinguish an audible observation from a sourced cultural claim.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is the first listening task?**

A. Identify a recurring pulse

B. Assign a dance tradition by appearance

C. Speed up the recording

D. Invent song history

**Key: A.** Identify a recurring pulse is the supported answer.

**2. What should a learner do before asserting historical origins?**

A. Guess from tempo

B. Check reliable sources and cultural context

C. Use an unsourced caption

D. Treat every bounce track as identical

**Key: B.** Check reliable sources and cultural context is the supported answer.

**3. How are audible accents handled?**

A. Assumed to be offbeats

B. Ignored entirely

C. Noted without assuming their metric position

D. Required on every count

**Key: C.** Noted without assuming their metric position is the supported answer.

**4. What is not provided by this lesson?**

A. An eight-count map

B. Rhythm marking

C. A practice alternative

D. Licensed music files

**Key: D.** Licensed music files is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
