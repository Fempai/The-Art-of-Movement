# TWK401 · Lesson 01 — Improvisation Prompts

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Create two distinct eight-count responses to the same pulse using deliberate movement choices.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with five minutes of gentle steps and comfortable pulses. Phrase A: over eight counts choose one pulse, one lateral shift and one pause; repeat twice. Phrase B: retain the same pulse but replace the lateral shift with a small pelvic orientation change and move the pause to count 8. Practice A–B–A–B with breaks, then describe how each choice changes the phrase.

## Accessible alternative
Seated pressure shifts and hand accents preserve choice and musical structure but change standing mechanics.

## Self-correction and safety
If improvisation feels overwhelming, choose only two actions. If movement becomes painful, reduce range or stop. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Notate A and B, naming one retained and one changed feature.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is the key improvisation constraint?**

A. Keep a shared pulse while changing choices

B. Use maximum speed

C. Copy a fixed routine exactly

D. Require floorwork

**Key: A.** Keep a shared pulse while changing choices is the supported answer.

**2. Which element changes in Phrase B?**

A. The beat disappears

B. The lateral shift is replaced

C. Every count doubles

D. The learner must kneel

**Key: B.** The lateral shift is replaced is the supported answer.

**3. What helps when choices feel overwhelming?**

A. Add five new moves

B. Stop listening

C. Limit the phrase to two actions

D. Increase intensity

**Key: C.** Limit the phrase to two actions is the supported answer.

**4. What must the reflection identify?**

A. Only song title

B. Only duration

C. Only repetitions

D. One retained and one changed feature

**Key: D.** One retained and one changed feature is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
