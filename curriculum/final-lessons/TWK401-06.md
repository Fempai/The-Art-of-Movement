# TWK401 · Lesson 06 — Performance Endurance and Recovery

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Plan repeated performance phrases with deliberate recovery rather than pushing through fatigue.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes. Perform a comfortable 16-count combination twice, then rest for 16 counts standing neutrally or seated. Repeat only if breathing and balance remain comfortable. In a second round, reduce movement range while preserving counts and compare control. Use an eight-count cooldown of gentle walking and easy breathing.

## Accessible alternative
Perform seated pressure shifts and hand accents, with the same planned rest interval.

## Self-correction and safety
If fatigue changes form, stop early. If dizziness occurs, stop immediately. Avoid treating a completion counter as a physical performance grade. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Write a 48-count work/rest plan and describe one pacing modification.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is the prescribed work/rest structure?**

A. Two 16-count phrases then 16 counts rest

B. 48 counts of maximal squats

C. No recovery

D. A continuous floor drop

**Key: A.** Two 16-count phrases then 16 counts rest is the supported answer.

**2. What changes in the second round?**

A. The meter

B. Movement range may be reduced

C. The requirement to rest disappears

D. The song must speed up

**Key: B.** Movement range may be reduced is the supported answer.

**3. What should happen if fatigue affects control?**

A. Push harder

B. Ignore it

C. Stop or shorten work

D. Increase range

**Key: C.** Stop or shorten work is the supported answer.

**4. What is the goal of the written plan?**

A. Prove muscle activation

B. Set a calorie target

C. Grade video appearance

D. Explain pacing and recovery choices

**Key: D.** Explain pacing and recovery choices is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
