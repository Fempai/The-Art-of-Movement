# TWK301 · Lesson 03 — Directional Travel

**Standalone advanced twerk pathway | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count phrase. Not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Link a small lateral step with a controlled pulse while keeping knee and foot direction aligned.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original movement study, not a named historical step. Modern twerk is associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; detailed cultural claims need verified sources and practitioner review before authoritative publication.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes with comfortable side steps and small pulses. Counts 1–2 step right and gather; 3–4 pulse and recover; 5–6 step left and gather; 7–8 pulse and recover. Repeat four phrases, pausing between pairs. Next perform the same pathway with accents on counts 3 and 7. Use small steps and a nonslip floor.

## Accessible alternative
Use counter-supported microsteps or seated directional reaches with pressure shifts; seated work does not reproduce traveling weight transfer.

## Self-correction and safety
If feet cross unintentionally, shorten steps. If knee twists, realign foot and knee. If rhythm rushes, remove accents. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Assignment
Notate two phrases, showing where travel ends and pulse begins.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. Which counts contain the rightward step and gather?**

A. 1–2

B. 3–4

C. 5–6

D. 7–8

**Key: A.** 1–2 is correct for the exercise.

**2. What happens on counts 3–4?**

A. A jump

B. Pulse and recovery

C. A spin

D. A floor drop

**Key: B.** Pulse and recovery is correct for the exercise.

**3. What helps when a step causes knee twisting?**

A. Increase travel

B. Lock the knee

C. Shorten steps and align foot and knee

D. Add speed

**Key: C.** Shorten steps and align foot and knee is correct for the exercise.

**4. What does seated reaching not reproduce?**

A. Directional intent

B. Count awareness

C. Upper-body accents

D. Traveling standing weight transfer

**Key: D.** Traveling standing weight transfer is correct for the exercise.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate two comfortable phrases, explain one timing choice and identify a correction. Written responses do not certify technique mastery.
