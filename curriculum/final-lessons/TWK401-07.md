# TWK401 · Lesson 07 — Original Choreography

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Build a 32-count original performance using rhythm, dynamics, direction and an accessible option.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up and review two comfortable foundation phrases. Phrase A (1–8): shallow pulse and side shift. Phrase B (9–16): same pathway with a pause on count 12. Phrase C (17–24): change direction using a small step and gather. Phrase D (25–32): return to center, add two chosen accents and finish in a comfortable still position. Rehearse each phrase separately, then join twice with rests. Replace any movement that causes discomfort.

## Accessible alternative
Use seated pressure changes, hand accents and directional reaches; document changed standing mechanics.

## Self-correction and safety
If sequence is lost, return to one eight-count section. If pain or fatigue arises, reduce range or rest. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Submit a 32-count score with phrase labels, dynamic contrast and one modification.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. How long is the choreography assignment?**

A. 32 counts

B. 8 counts

C. 12 counts

D. 64 counts

**Key: A.** 32 counts is the supported answer.

**2. Where is the pause in Phrase B?**

A. Count 9

B. Count 12

C. Count 16 only

D. Count 20

**Key: B.** Count 12 is the supported answer.

**3. What is the preferred response to losing sequence?**

A. Speed up

B. Add complexity

C. Rehearse one eight-count section

D. Force the full run

**Key: C.** Rehearse one eight-count section is the supported answer.

**4. Which submission meets the assignment?**

A. Only a video watch count

B. Only the track title

C. Only repetitions

D. A phrase-labeled score with contrast and modification

**Key: D.** A phrase-labeled score with contrast and modification is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
