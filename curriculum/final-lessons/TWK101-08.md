# TWK101 · Lesson 08 — Foundation Eight-Count Performance

**Standalone twerk foundations | learner-facing written pilot | 35–45 minutes.** This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Perform a short original phrase twice with controlled transitions and a justified adaptation.

## Cultural and technique context
Modern twerking is strongly associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce culture. Similar-looking actions across traditions should not be labeled identical. This is an original teaching exercise, not a named historical step. Historical sourcing and practitioner review are still pending.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up four minutes and review one comfortable example from each earlier unit. Compose an eight-count phrase using at least three of: small pulse, pelvic tilt, lateral shift, and deliberate accent. Practice slowly twice, then at a comfortable steady tempo twice, pausing between passes. Spend 10–15 minutes rehearsing and checking breathing, balance and recovery. No deep squat, forced back extension or fast impact is required.

## Accessible adaptation
Choose stable chair or counter support and design an eight-count phrase of pressure shifts, small pelvic rocks and upper-body accents. Name which standing actions are not reproduced.

## Self-correction and safety
Sequence breaks down: simplify to two action types. Fatigue: shorten to one phrase. Pain or instability: stop and choose a comfortable version. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Apply and reflect
Submit a count-by-count score or optional private recording; identify three actions, one modification, and a reason for your musical accents.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate two comfortable eight-count phrases; identify the movement initiator, one correction, and an adaptation. A written response does not prove physical proficiency.
