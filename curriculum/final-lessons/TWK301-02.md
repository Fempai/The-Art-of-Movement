# TWK301 · Lesson 02 — Quick-to-Slow Timing Contrast

**Standalone twerk advanced movement | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended prerequisite: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count combination. Not a validated instructor-led video.

## Objective
Contrast two comfortable phrase speeds without confusing faster timing with larger or more forceful movement.

## Cultural context
This original exercise explores musical and physical coordination. Twerk's relationships with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce warrant verified sources and practitioner review; no universal traditional step is claimed here.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with five minutes of walking, shallow leg pulses and slow pelvic orientation changes. Phrase A: over eight counts perform four shallow pulses, each using two counts for lowering and recovery. Phrase B: keep the same comfortable depth but mark four pulses on counts 1–4 and rest in a neutral stance on 5–8. Alternate A and B with at least eight counts of recovery between sets. Use a steady external beat and avoid maximal speed or impact.

## Accessible alternative
Seated pressure pulses and hand taps demonstrate tempo contrast without replicating standing leg demand.

## Self-correction and safety
If control degrades at quicker timing, reduce pulse count or return to Phrase A. If knees hurt, stop and use seated marking. If breathing is held, rest. Stop for pain, dizziness or loss of balance.

## Assignment
Write the count map for both phrases and explain why speed does not require more depth.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. How many pulses are in Phrase A?**

A. Four pulses across eight counts

B. Eight jumps

C. One sustained squat

D. Sixteen impacts

**Key: A.** Four pulses across eight counts.

**2. What happens on Phrase B counts 5–8?**

A. A forced drop

B. Rest in neutral stance

C. A full turn

D. A deep squat

**Key: B.** Rest in neutral stance.

**3. Which adjustment is appropriate when quicker timing reduces control?**

A. Add depth

B. Ignore fatigue

C. Reduce pulses or return to slower phrase

D. Increase force

**Key: C.** Reduce pulses or return to slower phrase.

**4. What does quicker timing not inherently require?**

A. A steady beat

B. A planned recovery

C. A comfortable stance

D. Greater movement depth

**Key: D.** Greater movement depth.

## Completion checkpoint
Demonstrate or notate two intentional eight-count phrases, explain the difference, and document one correction. Written work does not certify physical proficiency.
