# TWK301 · Lesson 07 — Sustained Phrase and Recovery

**Standalone advanced twerk pathway | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count phrase. Not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Pace a thirty-two-count sequence with planned recovery rather than maximizing repetitions.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original movement study, not a named historical step. Modern twerk is associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; detailed cultural claims need verified sources and practitioner review before authoritative publication.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up five minutes. Repeat a familiar eight-count pulse-and-shift phrase twice, then take eight counts of neutral standing or seated rest, followed by eight counts of slow accent marking. Repeat this 32-count block twice only if comfortable. Count aloud and monitor breath and balance; rest longer if needed. Quality and recovery are the goals, not endurance at any cost.

## Accessible alternative
Use chair-supported pressure shifts and hand accents for the entire block, keeping the same count map.

## Self-correction and safety
If the pulse becomes uncontrolled, shorten the active portion. If breathing becomes strained, rest. If balance changes, choose supported stance. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Assignment
Write the 32-count structure and identify the planned recovery window.
