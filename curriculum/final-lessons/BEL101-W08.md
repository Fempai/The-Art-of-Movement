# BEL101 · Friday Week 08 — Midterm: Hip Work and Hip Slide Reassessment

**Original planned video:** The 4 Types of Hip Work + Hip Slide – 5 Minute Workout

## Learning outcomes and concept

Revisit the original foundation videos and compare current controlled execution with an earlier record. This is a formative midterm, not a pass/fail demand for extreme range or a claim of certified technique. Identify weight support and timing, demonstrate the eight-count version twice, and describe one correction and one evidence limit.

## Follow-along practice

**Warm-up (4–5 minutes):** March or shift weight comfortably; move ankles and shoulders gently. Clear a nonslip space, use a stable fixed support as needed, and avoid forced rotation or painful range. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness, or loss of control.

**Count-by-count guided study (8–12 minutes):** 1–2 slide right and return; 3–4 slide left and return; 5–6 trace a small circle; 7–8 settle and identify one controlled improvement or remaining question.

**Repeat (5 minutes):** First say counts aloud at a slow tempo. Second focus on where your weight is supported. Third add a modest accent without increasing the movement range. Rest between attempts. When the original video link is restored, use it to compare the demonstrated vocabulary with this original drill, noting any difference rather than assuming equivalence.

**Modification:** Perform the phrase seated or at half speed, recording the adaptation rather than treating it as failure.

**Self-correction:** If your knees twist or your lower back compresses, reduce range and return to comfortable parallel stance. If the pulse disappears, mark counts with claps before moving again. Do not treat pain as evidence of productive practice.

**Success check:** Can you identify one observable difference between your first attempt and this attempt? **Worked observation:** Use counts, range, contact points, or transitions; avoid unverified claims about strength or safety. This checkpoint assesses self-observation, not medical safety or expert performance.

## Knowledge check — try before answers

**1. What is the midterm's primary purpose?**

A. Compare documented movement progress

B. Reach maximum hip range

C. Prove cultural authority

D. Perform through pain

**2. Which comparison is observable?**

A. A guaranteed injury reduction

B. More consistent timing over eight counts

C. A dancer's private intention

D. A medical diagnosis

**3. If the full phrase is uncomfortable, what should you do?**

A. Increase depth

B. Force a faster tempo

C. Use a smaller or seated version and document it

D. Skip the entire course

**4. Which conclusion is not justified by a video alone?**

A. The count sequence changed

B. The feet stayed grounded

C. The dancer reduced range

D. The exercise guarantees safe movement for everyone

### Answers and explanations

**1. A.** The midterm compares an earlier movement record with the present attempt using observable features such as count accuracy, support, and transitions; it does not demand maximum range.

**2. B.** Count alignment can be checked across two recorded attempts. Injury reduction, private intentions, and medical diagnoses cannot be established by this comparison.

**3. C.** A reduced-range or seated version keeps the timing-and-observation task accessible. Stop painful movement rather than forcing depth or speed.

**4. D.** A video may show counts, contact points, or range, but cannot prove that an exercise prevents injury or is safe for every learner.

**Retake rule:** For a missed question, revisit the concept, mark its relevant movement slowly, and retry. Four answers assess recall only; no psychometric or technical certification is claimed.

## Independent assignment

Record or write two eight-count attempts, identifying each count, supporting foot or seated surface, movement direction, and rhythmic accent. In 150–250 words compare an observable difference, one correction, one adaptation, and one uncertainty. Clearly distinguish your original practice from the named video technique, especially while the source URL remains unverified.

**Rubric:** movement pathway and counts 30%; self-correction 25%; modification and control 25%; careful cultural and source attribution 20%. Never grade maximal range or speed.
