# TWK301 · Lesson 05 — Supported Floor Entry and Exit

**Standalone advanced twerk pathway | written pilot | 40–50 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK201 sixteen-count phrase. Not a verified instructor-led video.

## Objective
Sequence a controlled optional descent and rise with stable supports; keep floorwork optional.

## Cultural and technique context
This is an original movement study, not a named historical step. Modern twerk is associated with Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; detailed cultural claims need verified sources and practitioner review before authoritative publication.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with five minutes of walking and comfortable hip mobility. First rehearse an eight-count standing phrase: 1–2 shallow pulse, 3–4 shift, 5–6 hinge and recover, 7–8 pause. If floor transitions are comfortable and a stable support and padded surface are available, rehearse a slow supported kneeling transition separately, using hands or furniture for balance and pausing at each stage; never drop onto knees. Return to standing using stable support. Practice 10 minutes including rests. Floor entry is not required for completion.

## Accessible alternative
Remain standing or seated throughout and mark the transition using a hand reach; this practices timing, not floor-entry mechanics.

## Self-correction and safety
Knee pressure or pain: avoid kneeling. Wrist discomfort: avoid weight-bearing on hands. Unsteadiness: remain upright with support. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Assignment
Choose either a supported transition or the no-floor alternative and justify your choice.
