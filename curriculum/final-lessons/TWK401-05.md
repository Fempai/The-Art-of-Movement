# TWK401 · Lesson 05 — Expressive Choices and Audience Attention

**Standalone twerk performance | learner-facing written pilot | 40–55 minutes.** Recommended: comfortable TWK301 combination. This is not a verified instructor-led video class.

## Objective
Choose a clear focal point and purposeful accent while preserving personal comfort and agency.

## Cultural and artistic context
This is an original performance exercise, not a claim to reproduce a named historical dance. Twerk has important relationships to Black social dance and New Orleans bounce; specific historical attributions need verified sources and practitioner review. No particular expression or audience interaction is mandatory.

## Follow-along practice
Warm up with easy standing or seated movement. Choose a comfortable focal point ahead of you, then practice an eight-count pulse-and-shift phrase while looking toward it. Repeat the same phrase but change the gaze to a second fixed point on count 5 and place a hand accent there. Compare the expressive effect without changing range. Eye contact with an audience is optional; avoid forced neck rotation.

## Accessible alternative
Remain seated, use small head or eye movements, or keep gaze fixed and vary a hand accent instead.

## Self-correction and safety
If neck tension appears, keep eyes forward. If self-conscious, practice without a camera or audience. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness or repeated loss of balance.

## Applied assignment
Describe two expressive versions and identify what changed without attributing emotions to observers.

## Four-question knowledge check

**1. What is the central expressive variable?**

A. Focal point and chosen accent

B. Maximum squat depth

C. Mandatory audience eye contact

D. Fastest tempo

**Key: A.** Focal point and chosen accent is the supported answer.

**2. When does the second version shift gaze?**

A. Count 1

B. Count 5

C. Count 8 only

D. Between every beat

**Key: B.** Count 5 is the supported answer.

**3. What should happen if neck tension occurs?**

A. Rotate farther

B. Hold the turn

C. Keep gaze forward

D. Add a head snap

**Key: C.** Keep gaze forward is the supported answer.

**4. What claim should the reflection avoid?**

A. Describing an accent

B. Noting a gaze choice

C. Comparing personal effects

D. Assuming the audience's internal emotions

**Key: D.** Assuming the audience's internal emotions is the supported answer.

## Completion checkpoint
Perform or notate the relevant phrases and explain musical choices, a correction and an adaptation. Neither a written answer nor an unreviewed recording establishes certified physical mastery.

## Production gate
Authorized instructor video, qualified technique review, captions, cultural-source verification and live browser acceptance are outstanding.
