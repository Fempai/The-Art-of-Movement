## How this track relates to the four BEL101 foundation lessons

- BEL101-01 covers grounding and introductory hip actions (Weeks 1–3).
- BEL101-02 covers small hip slides and circles; figure eights require their own explicit progression (Weeks 2, 7, 9).
- BEL101-03 covers a **preparatory** alternating-knee pulse, not a complete treatment of Egyptian shimmy technique (Weeks 6, 16).
- BEL101-04 covers original combination-making and cultural context. Weeks 10–15 require additional dedicated lessons on transitions, belly rolls, layering, arms, and undulations before the complete 16-week track can be considered implemented.

## Term structure and assessment

One Friday belly-dance class each week. Weeks 1–7 emphasize foundations and repeatable technique; Week 8 is a midterm check using the original hip-work and hip-slide clips. Weeks 9–15 build combinations and coordination, with movement scaled to comfort; Week 16 is a final comparison using the original hip-work and Egyptian-shimmy clips. Assess counts, repeatability, safe modifications, rhythmic awareness, and accurate cultural attribution. Avoid grading maximum range or speed.
