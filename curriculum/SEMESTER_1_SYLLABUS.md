# Semester 1 — Movement Literacy and Original Choreography

**Duration:** 12 flexible weeks. **Format:** Academic study outside gym; 20–30-minute dance practice blocks integrated with existing strength, treadmill, mobility and flexibility routine. **No accredited credits.**

## Weekly curriculum
### Week 1: Orientation & baseline
- **Academic theme:** MOV101 joint structure; MUS101 pulse; CMP101 motif.
- **Application / assignment:** Write baseline goals; score 8 counts of Undercurrent.
- **Reflection:** Identify one observation, one explanation, and one question for further investigation.
- **Technique gate:** Preserve pain-free alignment, clear timing, and controlled transitions; reduce complexity if these deteriorate.

## Reading pathway
Use the original introductory readings in `conservatory-learning-hub.html` for orientation. For formal academic rigor, supplement with properly cited scholarly texts and culturally grounded primary/secondary sources before treating modules as comprehensive.

## Practical support without a barre
Use a securely anchored waist-height rail or stable support with light fingertips. No moving treadmill or rolling chair. Center practice is acceptable at smaller range. Never force turnout from the knees/feet.

## Assessment
Academic: weekly responses, midterm, final reflection and research-backed explanation. Technical: emerging/developing/consistent/mastered based on repeatable control, alignment, timing, retention, recovery and safe technique. Creative: motif, revisions, annotated score, rehearsal journal and final work. Scores are separate; technical progression is not time-based.

## Accessibility and safety
Adapt for fatigue and available space. Inversions and advanced backbends require qualified supervision. Past range is not a safety clearance. Stop for pain, numbness, dizziness, or loss of control.
