# Instructional quality and quiz correctness review

Status: Self-study curriculum QA in progress. Structural quiz/browser checks are separate from content review. No external learner or instructor recruitment is required for this project's chosen release scope.

## Mandatory review for every lesson

1. Independently verify each factual, technical, and cultural claim against authoritative references; record evidence and uncertainty.
2. Answer all four quiz questions without viewing the key. Verify the keyed answer, explanation, source support, ambiguity, distractors, and alignment with taught material. Fix incorrect or ambiguous items.
3. Check beginner prerequisites, safe setup, starting position, weight support, movement pathway, counts, tempo, practice dosage, corrections, modifications, and stop conditions. Identify missing demonstrations.
4. Check progression from simple to complex practice and whether each objective has appropriate instruction, practice, feedback, and assessment. Distinguish knowledge recall from physical skill.
5. Conduct a desk-based novice walkthrough: follow each instruction literally, identify missing information, check self-correction cues, and revise confusing sequences. This is editorial usability review, not measured learning effectiveness.
6. Flag any advanced, high-risk, or discipline-specific claims that cannot be confidently checked against authoritative references; revise or clearly state limitations rather than inventing expert approval.
