# Instructional quality and quiz correctness review

Status: Protocol established. The 80 lessons and 320 quiz answers are NOT yet independently verified for factual correctness or teaching effectiveness.

## Mandatory review for every lesson

1. Independently verify each factual, technical, and cultural claim against authoritative references; record evidence and uncertainty.
2. Answer all four quiz questions without viewing the key. Verify the keyed answer, explanation, source support, ambiguity, distractors, and alignment with taught material. Fix incorrect or ambiguous items.
3. Check beginner prerequisites, safe setup, starting position, weight support, movement pathway, counts, tempo, practice dosage, corrections, modifications, and stop conditions. Identify missing demonstrations.
4. Check progression from simple to complex practice and whether each objective has appropriate instruction, practice, feedback, and assessment. Distinguish knowledge recall from physical skill.
5. Conduct consent-based independent beginner practice sessions, document confusion and performance, revise and retest. Do not claim effectiveness from author review alone.
6. Seek qualified dance-instructor review for technical movement accuracy and safety, and subject expertise for culturally specific claims.

## Review record

For each of MOV101, DNC101, CON101, HIP101, MUS101, CMP101, HIS101, LAB101, STG101, FLE101, LMA201, PER201, RES501, CMP502, SCI503, EST504, SIT505, PRO701, PRO702, PRO703, review lessons 01–04 individually. Record reviewer/date, claim evidence, independently checked answers, teaching completeness, objective alignment, beginner results, expert results, corrections, and unresolved risks.

Decisions: Not reviewed; Needs revision; Provisionally content-reviewed; Validated with learner and expert evidence. Never mark a lesson fully validated from structural parsing or browser tests.

## Release gate

All 320 quiz items must have independently checked keys and explanations; all 80 lessons must receive documented content and instructional review; material defects must be fixed. Learner and expert validation is required before claiming demonstrated teaching effectiveness. Re-run existing automated and deployed-site tests after edits.

Priority: foundations MOV101, then DNC101, followed by other beginner courses before advanced instruction. Character polish follows instructional quality.
