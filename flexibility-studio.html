Flexibility & Mobility Studio

Flexibility is a core pathway, not an afterthought. Develop usable range of motion for dance through gradual mobility, strength at range, balance and recovery. Track baseline measures, practice focused sessions, and compare progress over time.

Safety and measurement: Warm up before deeper range work. Stay within tolerable sensation, not sharp pain, numbness or joint instability. Avoid forced oversplits or assisted pushing. Measurements are self-recorded approximations, not medical tests or automatic certifications. Consult a qualified clinician if pain or injury affects training.

Weekly flexibility structure

Session A · Active range

20–30 minutes: gentle warm-up, controlled leg lifts, supported mobility, balance and cooldown. Keep ranges comfortable and stable.

Session B · Lower-body mobility

20–30 minutes: warmed hip flexors, hamstrings, adductors, ankle mobility and easy end-range isometrics. No forced splits.

Session C · Upper body & integration

20–30 minutes: thoracic rotation, shoulder mobility, controlled spinal articulation, then simple dance movement integration.

Progress through consistency and control, not a deadline for achieving splits or extreme ranges. Track pain-free motion and active control alongside passive range.
