Level I · Dance Foundations

A multi-discipline, technique-first dance education. You dance in Level I. Ballet and jazz establish core alignment, vocabulary, rhythm and coordination; modern/contemporary, hip-hop and belly dance introduce their own authentic foundations from the start. Each pathway progresses from guided technique through combinations and performance.

Technique focus

  1. Warm-up and comfortable joint preparation.
  2. Plié: coordinated hip, knee and ankle bending.
  3. Tendu and dégagé: articulated footwork and leg pathway.
  4. Rond de jambe and battement: leg control within comfortable range.
  5. Center combination: connect steps with musical counts.

Reflect: Which combination was easiest to follow? Where did your balance, timing or alignment change?

Curriculum architecture

Each discipline is planned as an eight-class introductory term. These are syllabus modules, not eight completed instructional videos. Later levels expand technical range, improvisation, choreography, performance, and style-specific specialization.

Later specializations

Heels, dancehall, twerk, burlesque/showgirl, breaking, krump, acro, floorwork specializations and other pathways will have their own introductory classes and prerequisites as appropriate. They are not random movements inserted into the general Level I syllabus. A learner may enter a beginner specialization once its own safety and technique prerequisites are met; completing all other dance styles is not required.

Use the Movement Lexicon for cultural dance names and anatomical terminology, the Dance Studio for local video playback tools, and the Pose Lab for experimental movement evidence.
