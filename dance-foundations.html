The Art of Movement · Curriculum

Dance Foundations

A structured introductory pathway for movement literacy, isolation, pelvic control and expressive practice. These are text-based lessons and drills, not a substitute for an instructor demonstration or an automated proficiency assessment. Follow the named movement in your chosen dance tradition.

Practice principles: Begin within a comfortable range. Stop if a movement causes pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms. For recorded assessments, select the intended movement name; a belly-dance omi and a dancehall wine have different stylistic goals.

Training journal and technique evidence

Record the session in your private cloud journal, study terms in the Movement Lexicon, and use Pose Lab for experimental camera-based movement evidence. Completion checkmarks on this page are stored only in this browser, not in the cloud.
