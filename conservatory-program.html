The Art of Movement · New curriculum

Conservatory Program

Learn to dance, compose, analyze, remember and perform through a progressively challenging original repertory. This is a self-directed academic-style program, not an accredited degree.

Foundation · Bachelor's-level breadth

Dance anatomy, alignment, strength, flexibility, ballet, jazz, contemporary, hip-hop, floorwork, musicality, Laban analysis, history, choreography and critical thinking.

Composition · Master's-level inquiry

Motif development, improvisation, narrative, abstraction, chance, research, performance theory and creative capstones.

Performance Laboratory · Professional-style

Repertory, stage presence, musical interpretation, stamina, performance refinement and supervised advanced skills.

First three detailed classes

  1. Choreographic Memory: Encode, Chunk, Retrieve — 45 min
  2. Choreography Composition: Motif to Narrative — 60 min
  3. Musicality: Rhythm, Silence and Syncopation — 50 min

Recurring original choreography: Undercurrent

Theme: restraint becoming freedom. Start with a simple eight-count reach-and-gather motif. Transform it through rhythm, levels, dynamics, narrative and increasingly complex spatial composition across the curriculum.

Dance conditioning and flexibility mini-lessons →

Advanced contortion and inversions require qualified supervision. Video instruction and credentialing are not included in this initial release.

Our adopted hybrid model

Academic terms + technical mastery + original choreography. Academic coursework follows flexible 12-week terms, while dance technique advances only through demonstrated safe control. Separate academic scores, technical rubrics and creative portfolios track different dimensions of learning.
