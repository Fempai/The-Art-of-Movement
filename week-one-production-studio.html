Week 1 · Instructor Video Production Studio

Production-ready specifications for the three unfinished demonstration needs. These are briefs, not completed instructional videos. For acro and flexibility, real qualified instruction is preferred to synthetic movement.

Active flexibility · Instructor demonstration needed

Target: 8–12-minute beginner follow-along. Demonstrate warmed-up supported front-leg lifts (2×6/side), lateral leg lifts (2×6/side), adductor rockbacks (2×6/side), and comfortable supported split approach (2×20 seconds). Include frontal and lateral camera angles, spoken alignment cues, left/right modifications, no forced range, and stop-for-pain instructions.

Status: Not sourced, filmed, or safety reviewed. Do not treat written instructions as a replacement for demonstration.

Acro readiness · Instructor demonstration needed

Target: 8–10-minute qualified-instructor tutorial. Wrist circles, graded palm/wrist loading, wall slides, quadruped weight shifts, dead bug control. Show beginner regressions and contraindication reminders. No unsupervised inversions, balances, or tricks.

Status: Not sourced, filmed, or safety reviewed.

32-count choreography · AI visualization pilot

Produce four separate eight-count clips, then stitch into one continuous lesson. Preserve one dancer, same wardrobe, fixed full-body framing, visible feet, stable room, continuous beat, and natural joint anatomy. AI footage is a visual rehearsal reference, not authoritative movement instruction until reviewed against the actual counts.

Global direction: One adult dance demonstrator in a bright uncluttered studio, full body and feet visible throughout, fixed wide frontal camera, comfortable beginner technique, clear neutral alignment, no extreme flexibility or acrobatics, natural anatomy, consistent clothing, no camera cuts, eight steady beats per clip.

Clip 1 · Counts 1–8: comfortable plié with controlled rise and neutral knee-foot tracking.
Clip 2 · Counts 9–16: four alternating jazz walks with grounded, deliberate weight transfers.
Clip 3 · Counts 17–24: beginner hip-hop bounce and rock with soft knees and clear rhythmic pulse.
Clip 4 · Counts 25–32: counts 25–28 gentle contemporary reach and release; counts 29–32 controlled hip slides without twisting the knees.

Postproduction: Add audible or visible 1–8 count cues, chapter labels, a four-panel count reference, and optional slowed playback. Review continuity, counts, foot placement, and anatomical accuracy before publishing.

Suggested workflow: Generate clips in Flow or Runway, inspect each eight-count segment, then edit into one lesson. Avoid presenting AI footage as a qualified instructor's demonstration.
