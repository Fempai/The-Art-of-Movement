Movement Lexicon

Learn the names dancers actually use, their stylistic traditions, and the biomechanics behind each movement. Related movements across traditions are not automatically interchangeable.

Language matters. Belly-dance naming varies by teacher and lineage. Omi (also umi) commonly describes an interior hip circle, while a dancehall wine/whine is a culturally distinct movement family. A broad hip circle is not always an omi. Terms here are contextual, not universal certifications.

How the instructor uses this vocabulary

Every skill assessment must first identify the specific style and named movement. A belly-dance omi is assessed against an interior-circle rubric, not automatically against an exterior hip circle or dancehall wine. Movement terminology is shown alongside the relevant technical description; the AI may only evaluate characteristics supported by visible evidence.

Pose tracking from one camera cannot directly prove deep muscle engagement, joint loading, or full three-dimensional motion. Formal AI grades remain under development.
