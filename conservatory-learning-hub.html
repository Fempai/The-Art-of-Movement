Theme: restraint becoming freedom. The original motif returns in increasingly complex versions.

Write your movement score

Use five columns: counts/time · body action · facing/pathway · musical cue · emotion/imagery. Capture spontaneous movement first in a private note or voice memo, then reconstruct counts and transitions. Test recall tomorrow, after three days and after seven days.

Starter phrase: 1–2 step right and settle; 3–4 gather arms and draw foot in; 5–6 diagonal reach and torso rotation; 7–8 recover and suspend.

Do not add extreme kicks, unsupported inversions or contortion shapes simply to meet a level number.
