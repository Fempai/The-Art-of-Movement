TERM I · WEEK 2 · FOUNDATION DEVELOPMENT

Control Before Range

Week 2 builds on Week 1 with clean weight transfers, grounded rhythm, controlled mobility, and clear phrasing. This is a structured practice plan, not a claim that all new instructor videos are sourced.

Training boundaries: Ballet remains a short reentry class (about 25 minutes, first 10 required); gym workouts remain independent and written, not video classes. Daily gentle mobility, two to three controlled flexibility sessions, and low-risk acro preparation. No forced splits, bridges, inversions, or unsupported tricks.

Weekly targets

Mark four clean eight-count phrases; transfer weight without losing balance; repeat controlled active leg lifts at a comfortable range; distinguish hip slides from knee rotation; and perform a 32-count phrase twice with consistent counts. Assess technique and comfort, not maximum depth.

Week 2 musicality assignment

Jessie Ma · Rhythm & Musicality 1 is assigned this week, not Week 1. Watch the embedded class and apply its counting approach to your traveling phrase.

Seven-day studio plan

Monday · Ballet + gym full body

Alignment and footwork

Repeat Kathryn Morgan's beginner ballet class: first 10 minutes required, remaining time optional up to approximately 25 minutes. Practice plié, tendu, and neutral tracking. Independent gym: full-body/glute reentry per your established five-day split, with cardio at the end.

Ballet instructor video → · Gym log →

Daily mobility: 5–8 minutes easy joint movement

Tuesday · Upper A + active mobility

Control at comfortable range

After a warm-up: supported front leg lift 2×6 each side, lateral leg lift 2×6 each side, adductor rockback 2×6. Keep ribs stacked and avoid chasing height. Follow independent Upper A gym prescription; cardio after strength.

Instructor-led beginner active flexibility video still required; written drills are interim only

Wednesday · Jazz, hip-hop + lower A

Rhythm and grounded transfers

Review Week 1 jazz walk mechanics and hip-hop Bounce, Rock & Skate; practice four groups of eight at an easy tempo. Gym: glutes/lower A; cardio at end. Mobility: gentle calves and hips, 5–10 minutes.

Thursday · Contemporary + Upper B

Release without collapse

Review contemporary warm-up and routine. Mark the same phrase once slowly, once at regular pace. Add 10 minutes gentle full-body stretch after movement. Independent Upper B gym; cardio after lifting.

Friday · Belly isolations + lower B

Independent hip control

Review the four types of hip work. Practice controlled side-to-side slides 2×8 and gentle hip lifts 2×8, maintaining stable knees. Independent glutes/lower B gym; cardio after lifting. Gentle recovery stretch, 5–10 minutes.

Saturday · Acro preparation + musicality

Wrist, shoulder, trunk and accents

Acro preparation: wrist circles, gentle quadruped weight shifts, wall slides, and dead bug control; no acro tricks. Complete Jessie Ma · Rhythm & Musicality 1, then select two accent patterns to apply to a 32-count phrase. Mobility 5–10 minutes.

A verified free adult-appropriate acro video is still needed before calling this a filmed class

Sunday · Recovery and assessment

Repeatable movement

Gentle 5–10-minute mobility. Record one 32-count phrase twice. Note count consistency, right/left control, fatigue, soreness and modifications. Stop or reduce intensity if pain persists.

Daily movement ritual

Every day: start with 3–5 minutes easy dynamic joint motion before practice. Finish with 5–10 minutes comfortable stretching or mobility, depending on fatigue. Longer flexibility sessions belong after warming up, not before maximal lifting. Week 2 is a technique consolidation week, not a flexibility intensity jump.

Week 2 readiness gate

Move on when counts remain consistent, controlled ranges are pain-free, and recovery is manageable. Otherwise repeat or modify Week 2 rather than advancing range or acro complexity.
