Ballet I · Full Studio Class

A traditional 150-minute dance-school architecture. Conditioning and active flexibility prepare the body; barre introduces technique; center tests balance; traveling uses the same movement vocabulary; the final phrase integrates technique and music.

Teaching template, not a completed filmed class. The linked 25-minute Kathryn Morgan lesson is an initial source, not enough footage to fill 150 minutes. Each segment needs a matched instructor demonstration and real playback review. Begin with a shorter return-to-dance dose and build toward this length as recovery permits, especially while also pursuing five gym sessions.

Progression rules

Week 1: neutral alignment, comfortable plié, tendu, foot articulation, weight shifts and controlled travel. Across-the-floor movement is walking, chassé preparation and simple directional patterns, not forced leaps. Week 2: articulate and transfer weight more precisely before adding complexity. Record front and side technique samples and log joint symptoms, fatigue and quality.
