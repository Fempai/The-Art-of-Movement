      Cross-device acceptance checklist

      1. On your first browser, sign in through My Conservatory. Mark one real written lesson reviewed and complete one knowledge check. Note the lesson IDs and scores.
      2. Return here and select Refresh cloud verification. Confirm both records appear.
      3. In a separate browser profile or device, sign in with the same Academy account. Open this page and confirm the same records appear. Compare the SHA-256 progress fingerprints on both devices. Matching fingerprints confirm the same lesson IDs and scores were retrieved without relying on local browser storage.
      4. Sign out of the second browser. Confirm the account's cloud records disappear from this page.
      5. If you have a separate authorized test account, sign into it in the second browser and verify that the first account's records do not appear. Never share sign-in codes or credentials.

      Account separation is also enforced by database row-level security. This checklist verifies real browser behavior in addition to those database checks.
