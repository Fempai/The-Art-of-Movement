Your Movement Academy: Term I

Updated: This is the earlier eight-week source shortlist. The full first-semester curriculum is now 16 weeks. Paid resources here are comparisons only, never required coursework.

Curated real instructors and full classes, sequenced across performance flexibility, ballet, jazz, contemporary, hip-hop, belly dance, strength and safe acro preparation. This is a flexible eight-week pilot, not the entire academy curriculum.

Important: The 19 resources below are sourced class/program links, not 19 videos copied into our academy. Free, paid and signup access are marked. Some paid references are optional substitutes, and some resource pages contain multiple classes. I verified the published descriptions and lesson structures, but did not personally watch every class end to end or test every player. No instructor-level AI assessment is live yet. An eight-week plan cannot guarantee splits, inversions or a particular dance level.

How the term works

Choose 3–5 practice days weekly based on recovery and time. Include two focused performance-flexibility sessions separated by recovery, two dance-technique days, one or two strength sessions, and short optional acro-prep blocks. You do not need to perform all five dance styles in every week; rotate styles and keep the progression in your chosen focus. Paid classes are not required coursework. The academy must offer a no-cost route through each discipline.

Assessment checkpoints

Week 1: optional front/side/back baseline, supported front/middle split measurements, active leg lift, balance, and one short dance phrase. Week 4: repeat selected recordings with matched camera and warm-up. Week 8: retest and review control, consistency, musicality and movement quality. The existing Pose Lab can supply experimental landmark evidence; validated timestamped movement-specific AI feedback remains in development.

Zero-cost policy and first-term gaps

Paid external resources are comparison references only, not academy requirements. Already-owned classes may be accessed privately but not copied or rehosted without permission. Missing dedicated instruction includes musicality, footwork, turns, jumps, traveling, choreography memory, anatomy, recovery and validated advancement assessments. This eight-week pilot is not yet a complete beginner-to-intermediate term.

What full production still requires

Rights-cleared media files or approved external players, tested captioned instruction, movement-specific AI feedback, learner-level branching, progress syncing and expert review of acro and deep-flexibility instruction. Links alone do not fulfill the original ad-free, instructor-led production requirement.
