Complete First-Semester Academy Curriculum

Sixteen weeks of progressive technique, strength, dancer/acro flexibility, musicality, choreography and performance literacy. Free-first and no required purchases. Rotate the five dance styles rather than attempting every full class every day.

Production status: This is a complete semester syllabus and skill map, not 16 weeks of licensed follow-along videos. Each week still requires instructor-led source matching, playback testing and a reviewed teaching sequence. Existing Pose Lab feedback is experimental and is not validated grading.

Weekly training structure

Two technique days

Choose a primary style (ballet, jazz, contemporary, hip-hop or belly dance), plus one rotating secondary discipline. Practice fundamental drills and one guided class.

Two performance-flexibility sessions

Goal-specific front/middle splits, active leg extensions or backbend readiness. Include active end-range strength and adequate recovery between demanding sessions.

One or two strength blocks

Balance, trunk control, hinge/squat, calf/foot, unilateral strength and progressive load appropriate to experience. These can share days with shorter technique sessions.

One creative/assessment block

Musicality, freestyle, choreography memory, video review and journal reflection. Add rest days as needed; do not train through pain or significant fatigue.

Advancement rubric: beginner → early intermediate

Technique

Repeatable alignment, controlled plié, tendu, weight transfer, basic turns and low-impact landing mechanics without pain or major compensation.

Musicality

Find the beat, count an eight, recognize phrase changes, match basic accents and recover after losing counts.

Learning and performance

Learn, retain and perform a 16–32-count beginner phrase with appropriate timing, transitions, spatial awareness and intentional dynamics.

Strength and mobility

Demonstrate active range with control, balanced left/right training, safe movement transitions and appropriate recovery. Full splits are not an advancement prerequisite.

Reflection and feedback

Compare standardized videos, explain one technical strength and one improvement target, and choose a next practice focus. AI landmark observations are supplementary until validated.

Acro safety

Understand prerequisites, equipment and spotting. Advanced inversions and tumbling are coach-supervised and are not graduation requirements.

How personal course purchases fit

If you already own Fit & Bendy or another contortion program, you may open and use your purchased classes personally from their authorized source. The academy can link to the original access page and track your own practice notes; copying course videos to our cloud player requires the rights holder's redistribution authorization, even when the app is private.
