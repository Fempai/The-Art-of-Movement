What the overlay can and cannot tell you

It can mark approximate visible body landmarks frame by frame. Occlusion, unusual angles, floorwork, fast spins, loose clothing, and camera position can distort tracking. It does not directly measure muscle activation, detect injury, or certify movement quality. Facial-only footage may not provide enough body landmarks for a meaningful overlay.

For privacy, your video stays in your browser. The pose model and library load from an external CDN. Instructor evaluation is a separate server-side feature; no AI grade is issued here.
