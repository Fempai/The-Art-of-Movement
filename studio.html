Dance curriculum · planned tracks

Heels and sensual floorwork · burlesque and showgirl · belly dance · twerk and glute isolations · contemporary and jazz · hip-hop and musicality · feminine expression and performance psychology.

These are curriculum categories, not finished instructor-led courses. Psychological reflection and optional spiritual practices will be clearly distinguished from evidence-based physical instruction, with secular alternatives.

Monday's gentle dance phrase (optional, 5–8 min)

  1. 60 sec side-to-side weight shifts with soft knees.
  2. 2 min controlled lateral hip slides, alternating sides.
  3. 2 min small horizontal hip figure-eights.
  4. 1 min ribcage isolation without forcing the low back.
  5. 1–2 min combine to slow music; pause when fatigued.

Do not attempt advanced heels, drops, splits or floor tricks without instruction and an appropriate practice surface.

Assessment and progress

Automated posture grading, instructor feedback, choreography scoring, progress-photo storage and year-over-year video montages are not active yet. A future consent-based review workflow will require secure authentication, private media storage and qualified reviewers. The player does not analyze or transmit your video.
