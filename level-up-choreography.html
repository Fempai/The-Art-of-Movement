01 · Beginner

Grounded bounce, step-touch, weight transfer, single-layer arm hits, low-impact substitutions and short eight-count phrases.

02 · Intermediate

Faster footwork, direction changes, arm/torso layering, precise accents and sustained sequences.

03 · Full performance

Reconstruct visible source movement, match musical phrasing and bridge camera-cut gaps with clearly marked connecting counts.

Beginner preparatory session · 15 minutes

  1. 3 min: warm-up and ankle/shoulder mobility
  2. 4 min: alternating step-touch with full weight transfer
  3. 4 min: grounded bounce plus isolated arm hits
  4. 4 min: practice phrase at half speed then controlled full speed

Training phrase (not source choreography): 1 step right · 2 touch left · 3 step left · 4 touch right · 5 bounce · 6 bounce · 7 arm accent · 8 hold. Repeat and reverse direction.

Source analysis checklist

  • Mark visible phrase start/end and music timestamps.
  • Record foot placement, weight-bearing leg, arm path, facing and level.
  • Compare cuts for continuation versus different take.
  • Insert connecting counts only where source movement is not shown.
  • Check whether adaptations preserve musical entrances and phrase lengths.
  • Film and review from front and side; never assume an AI rendering proves physical correctness.

16-week placement

Weeks 1–4: foundations · Weeks 5–8: beginner adaptation · Weeks 9–12: intermediate adaptation · Weeks 13–15: source choreography and connecting transitions · Week 16: performance assessment.
