One Body. Many Movement Languages.

Weekly outcomes

Demonstrate neutral stance and a comfortable plié; articulate feet and transfer weight; maintain a bounce/rock pulse; distinguish jazz walks, contemporary release and belly-dance isolations; and perform a simple 32-count diagnostic phrase. Document left/right active hip control, not maximum split depth.

Gym strength · Week 1 · Body composition and performance

Your goals are weight loss, muscle gain, greater strength, glute development and improved dance performance. The gym plan is an independent training track, not a substitute for dance or flexibility. This first week is a controlled return-to-training microcycle. It builds toward your preferred three lower-body/glute and two upper-body training days, with a possible third upper-body day once recovery supports it. Cardio stays at the end of every lifting session, as in your Monday routine.

Monday · Full-body re-entry, glute emphasis

Preparation: 4–6 min. Leg press 2×8–10; glute bridge 2×10–12; seated cable row 2×8–10; chest press machine 2×8–10; optional supported calf raise 1×10. Rest 60–120 seconds. Mobility: adductor rock-backs 2×6/side, half-kneeling hip flexors, strap hamstrings and supported calves 2×20–30 sec/side. Cardio last: 8–12 min easy treadmill/bike. Optional 5–8 min hip isolations.

Tuesday · Upper-body A

Seated cable row 2×8–10; chest press machine 2×8–10; neutral-grip lat pulldown 2×8–10; lateral raise 2×10–12; cable or machine triceps pressdown 2×10–12. Cardio last: 8–12 min easy. Dance class and comfortable shoulder mobility can be separated by several hours.

Wednesday · Glutes/lower A

Leg press 2×8–10; machine or dumbbell hip thrust 2×10–12; seated hamstring curl 2×10–12; supported step-up 2×8/side; seated calf raise 2×10–12. Cardio last: 8–12 min easy. Use light loads this first week.

Thursday · Upper-body B

Chest-supported row 2×8–10; incline chest press machine 2×8–10; shoulder press machine 2×8–10 (comfortable range); cable biceps curl 2×10–12; face pull 2×10–12. Cardio last: 8–12 min easy. Reduce shoulder work if acro preparation or contemporary floorwork creates fatigue.

Friday · Glutes/lower B

Glute bridge or hip thrust 2×8–10; supported reverse lunge 2×8/side; light Romanian deadlift 2×8–10; seated hip abduction 2×12; calf raise 2×10–12. Cardio last: 8–12 min easy. Prioritize clean hip hinge and controlled eccentric movement.

Saturday · Optional technique / upper-body C later

Week 1: recovery, walking and dance technique rather than a sixth lifting day. After the initial return-to-training phase, a third upper-body day may be added if soreness, joints and recovery permit. No extra gym work is mandatory in Week 1.

Progression and safety: Week 1 uses about 3–4 repetitions in reserve and 60–120 seconds of rest. Log loads, sets, repetitions, perceived effort and discomfort in Strength Log. Progress toward harder sets and more total work gradually in later weeks. These are suggested training days, not a requirement to train through pain, severe soreness or poor recovery. If five lifting sessions is too much after time away, use Monday plus two additional sessions and build up.

Scheduling note: The dance lessons above are skill assignments and can be done in shorter blocks before/after the gym or on different days. This Week 1 timetable is a gym training track alongside the academy classes, not a replacement for them.
