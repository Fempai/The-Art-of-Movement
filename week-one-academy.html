TERM I · WEEK 1 · FOUNDATION

One Body. Many Movement Languages.

Week-one sequence: posture → plié → weight transfer → rhythm → expression. Ballet introduces alignment; strength stabilizes it; jazz changes movement quality; hip-hop applies weight transfer to pulse; contemporary explores release; belly dance isolates segments; flexibility and acro readiness build controlled range; musicality integrates everything.

Status: Complete week-one syllabus, practice instructions, assessments and source assignments. Not a complete filmed week. Weekly outcomes

Demonstrate neutral stance and a comfortable plié; articulate feet and transfer weight; maintain a bounce/rock pulse; distinguish jazz walks, contemporary release and belly-dance isolations; and perform a simple 32-count diagnostic phrase. Document left/right active hip control, not maximum split depth.

Assessment and next-week prerequisites

Record 20–40 seconds of plié and weight transfer, jazz walk, hip-hop groove, hip isolation and the integrated phrase. Note knee/foot tracking, balance, beat and repeatability. Full splits and acro tricks are not prerequisites. Upload optional baselines, document observations, and review experimental AI landmarks with uncertainty. Week 2 builds on these same movements rather than introducing unrelated choreography.
