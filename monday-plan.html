Your Monday Session

First week back: controlled strength + mobility. About 45–60 minutes total, with permission to shorten it. Your goal is to finish feeling capable of returning.

Given your past joint pain, stiffness and possible hypermobility, use a comfortable, controlled range. Don't lock your knees, bounce into stretches, or chase maximum depth. If joint pain, breathlessness, dizziness or unusual symptoms return, stop and seek clinical advice. A hypermobility-informed physical therapist can help assess safe end ranges.

1 · Warm-up (7–10 min)

Keep the effort conversational.

2 · Strength (25–30 min)

Use a weight that allows 3–4 more controlled repetitions at the end of each set. Rest 60–120 seconds as needed. If equipment is unavailable, skip or substitute a comfortable equivalent.

Do not force a deep squat or wide stance. Record machine settings and loads in the Training Journal.

3 · Flexibility and mobility (12–18 min)

Move slowly at mild-to-moderate tension, never sharp pain. Keep the joints active and supported.

Longer 30–45-minute dedicated flexibility sessions can be added later on separate days as tolerated; Monday is deliberately introductory.

4 · Finish (3–5 min)
