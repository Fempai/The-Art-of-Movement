Your first session

Strength & movement orientation

Begin with 5–10 minutes of comfortable walking or cycling. Practice a light full-body session with controlled technique, leaving several repetitions in reserve. Finish with gentle hip and ankle mobility. Keep the entire first session manageable, about 35–50 minutes.

For your first week, prioritize learning the movements over lifting heavy. Stop any drill that causes sharp pain, instability or dizziness. Do not attempt acrobatic or contortion skills without a qualified in-person coach.

Strength

Leg press or supported squat, seated row, chest press, glute bridge. Start with 1–2 light sets each; rest as needed.

Mobility

Controlled hip circles, supported adductor rock-backs and gentle calf mobility. Avoid forcing end-range positions.

Dance

Easy musicality and isolation practice, without aggressive stretching or maximal kicks.

Movement Baseline

Track comfortable movement quality, not how far you can force a joint. Assess hip control, shoulder control, balance and squat comfort. Different areas may progress at different rates.
