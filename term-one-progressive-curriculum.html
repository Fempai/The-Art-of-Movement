THE ART OF MOVEMENT · TERM I

Sixteen Weeks of Movement

Progressive curriculum, Weeks 1–16. Each week advances control, coordination and musical interpretation before adding complexity. This is a complete written term map, not 16 weeks of verified filmed classes. Weeks 1 and 2 have dedicated daily lesson pages; future instructor video sourcing and audits are ongoing.

Daily: 3–5 minutes dynamic warm-up plus 5–10 minutes gentle mobility or comfortable post-session stretching. Weekly: five independent gym sessions, two or three structured active flexibility practices as recovery permits, one or two short acro preparation blocks, and at least one rest/recovery day. No mandatory splits, bridges, handstands, or tumbling.

Progression rule: repeat or modify a week if pain, poor control or fatigue disrupts technique. Advanced Fit & Bendy material is not a foundation-level requirement.
